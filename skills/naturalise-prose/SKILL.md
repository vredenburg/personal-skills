# Naturalise prose

Make existing prose feel specific, organic, and native to its intended voice. Improve the writing itself; do not try to prove human authorship or optimise for an AI detector. If detector evasion is requested, offer an ordinary editorial revision without promising a detector outcome.

## Establish the edit contract

Infer the audience, register, desired editing strength, and whether the text is fiction or nonfiction from the request and surrounding material. Use a supplied voice sample, style guide, or explicitly in-scope canon as a constraint. Ask a question only when unresolved ambiguity would materially change the edit.

Default to the least intervention that solves the stated problem. An intensive line edit may change sentence and paragraph construction when requested, but developmental changes to argument, plot, characterization, or structure fall outside this skill unless separately authorized.

## Preserve before revising

Treat these as invariants unless the user explicitly asks to change them:

- meaning, factual claims, evidential strength, uncertainty, and the author's position;
- names, numbers, chronology, citations, quotations, technical terms, and meaningful formatting;
- register, point of view, tense, narrative distance, character knowledge, and project canon;
- intentional repetition, fragments, metaphors, rhetorical questions, punctuation, and other voice-bearing choices.

Never invent facts, sources, examples, experiences, opinions, emotions, motives, sensory detail, or lore to make prose seem more vivid or personal. Specificity must come from the source or other authorized, verified context. If a vague claim cannot be made concrete safely, retain its attribution and uncertainty, flagging missing support when useful. Missing support alone does not justify deletion; remove a claim only when redundant without loss of meaning or when the user's requested content cuts authorize its removal.

When matching a voice sample, borrow high-level tendencies such as syntax, diction, rhythm, punctuation, and paragraph movement. Do not import the sample's content or exaggerate its quirks into caricature.

## Choose the mode

- **Fiction:** protect POV, tense, narrative distance, character diction, scene logic, pacing, ambiguity, imagery, and canon. For passage-level fiction edits or close voice matching, read [references/fiction-editing.md](references/fiction-editing.md) to guide recasting and compare its effect with the original. Do not normalize purposeful irregularity or add interiority and detail that the author did not supply.
- **Nonfiction:** protect claims, attribution, qualifications, citations, quotations, terminology, and document structure. Do not replace vague authority with an invented source or remove qualifiers required for accuracy.

For passage-level or long-form work, close voice or canon matching, or uncertain diagnostic calls, read [references/editorial-rubric.md](references/editorial-rubric.md). A small, obvious sentence-level fix does not require loading the rubric.

## Revise contextually

Read enough surrounding text to understand its purpose and cadence before editing individual sentences. Diagnose expressions by what they do in context, not by isolated vocabulary or punctuation.

Look for clusters of empty elevation, generic abstraction, canned transitions, formulaic contrasts, repeated rhetorical frames, redundant explanation, unsupported authority, synonym cycling, decorative metaphor, and mechanical cadence. Revise only where a habit obscures meaning, repeats conspicuously, or substitutes ceremony for substance. A familiar word or device is not a defect by itself.

Prefer targeted recasting over global stylistic normalization. Recover the author's own texture rather than imposing a default conversational, minimalist, or "opinionated" voice. Do not add mistakes, random variation, slang, or inconsistent typography as artificial signs of humanness.

Before delivering, check the revision against the agreed edit contract. New propositions must be supported and within the requested scope. Preserve nonredundant claims, necessary qualifications, narrative functions, canonical details, quotations, citations, and deliberate stylistic choices except where changes were explicitly requested.

## Return the result

For a rewrite request, provide one polished revision by default. Add a compact explanation of meaningful changes only when useful or requested; do not expose a draft, detector score, or scripted self-critique.

For review-only requests, identify the consequential patterns and explain why they weaken this text without rewriting it. For file edits, change only the scoped passages and summarize or show the relevant changes.
