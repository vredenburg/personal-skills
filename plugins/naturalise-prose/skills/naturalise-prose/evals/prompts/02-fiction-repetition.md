## Scene

Iona stopped on the landing. One step below the door. One step beyond the last window.

"You saw him leave?" she asked.

"Didn't say that," Hal said. "Said his coat was gone."

From where she stood, Iona could see the hook beside the door. Empty. The kitchen light showed under the threshold, a thin, unwavering line.

For a moment, she found herself considering the possibility that Hal had moved the coat. He had a key. So did Mara.

"You may go in," he said.

"May I."

She did not reach for the handle.
