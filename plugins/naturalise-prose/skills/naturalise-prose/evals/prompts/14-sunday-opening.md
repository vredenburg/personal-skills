On Sundays Vera opens the museum at ten. She turns the sign to OPEN, although anyone close enough to read it can already see her through the glass. Then she winds the clock in the entrance hall. It has been eleven minutes fast for as long as she has worked here. Visitors find this charming. Vera would prefer eleven minutes slow.

Today there is a postcard under the door. Her brother has sent it from a town with a castle, and the castle fills the photograph so completely that the town might be a rumour. On the back he has written, You'd like it here. He has said this about every place he has visited, including the airport hotel where he caught food poisoning.

She puts the card beside the till and begins the process of taking the dust sheet off the model railway. One of the little sheep has fallen over during the night. She sets it on its feet, outside the fence, where it seems to have been going. At ten past ten the first visitor arrives and asks if she has opened yet.
